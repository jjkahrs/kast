Tests

  • chat_self_check (ctest --preset debug in services/chat; also run by the Docker build): the chat messages' codec, binding (held, replaced, dropped on CharOffline and a lost link), whispers across maps and to offline names, channels (General on connect, join/leave Trade, non-members), the zone channel following map changes, ignore / unignore, mutes (players refused, GM mute persisted and sent to the links, notices, expiry, unmute, cap), the rate limit and text rules, the config, and token rejection. No network or database: time is passed in.
  • world_self_check (services/world): the v8 codec (every chat and link message, kinds, malformed values), proximity chat (say in view, yell past the view radius but not past CHAT_YELL_RADIUS nor to another map, emote, muted characters dropped with a notice, expiry) and the chat config.
  • Integration (services/world/tests/integration.sh, against the compose stack): kast_bot scenarios 29-38:
    • 29 yell: say to B 100 m away only; yell reaches C 200 m away (past the 128 m view radius), not D 320 m away; emote.
    • 30 chat-whisper: ChatServer after EnterWorldOk; a whisper reaches B on the cave map; an offline name gets not_online.
    • 31 chat-channels: General on connect; Trade join, members only, not_in_channel, leave.
    • 32 chat-zone: zone lines stay on the sender's map and follow portals.
    • 33 chat-ignore: whisper, channel and zone lines of an ignored sender never arrive; unignore restores them.
    • 34 chat-mute: a player can't mute; a GM (the script gives the account role gm in auth's database) mutes B for a minute: B's channel line and its say, yell and emote are dropped with notices; docker compose restart chat; still muted (from MySQL); the mute expires; mute and unmute.
    • 35 chat-limits: control characters stripped, oversize and invalid UTF-8 rejected, 5 per 10 s, no disconnect.
    • 36 chat-auth: garbage, tampered and expired tokens; a valid token without a character (held, then no_character); a wrong link secret, a wrong link version and link messages before LinkHello.
    • 37 chat-down: docker compose stop chat: chat connections close, say still works; start: clients reconnect, General again, whispers work.
    • 38: no chat text of these scenarios in the chat service's log.
  • Load: kast_bot --mode load --behaviour chat (say) still runs against the stack.
  • Engine (kast_self_check --net, root ctest engine_self_check): ChatClient against a fake chat service (tests/net_test_util.*): it connects where world.chatServer says, every request and event, the world server's muted-say chat.error, the service shutting down and refusing (one chat.status, requests throw), the reconnect with backoff rejoining Trade, and world.disconnected ending it.
  • Starter MMO (root ctest starter_mmo_live_chat, starter_mmo_live_chat_down): slash commands typed into the sample's chat box, colour-tagged lines, real Enter; with the chat service stopped, the box says it is unavailable and say still works. Screenshots in build/<preset>/starter_mmo/.

services/world/tests/docs_check.sh also checks that every chat opcode and notice code is in Protocol and every chat environment variable in these pages.
