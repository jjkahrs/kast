Tests

  • Self-check (tests/self_check.cpp + tests/lua_checks.cpp, ctest world_self_check): assert-style checks of the pure logic with an injected clock, so nothing sleeps. The Lua checks write their scripts to a temp folder and drive a World with captured sends, saves and log lines. They cover the event bus (the engine's cases), kast.publish/subscribe/unsubscribe and the client sends: audiences, order, rejections and timing. They also cover state machines, from definitions to C++ / Lua parity.
  • Client events (self_check.cpp + lua_checks.cpp): the ClientEvent codec round trip, its exact bytes and opcode name; client_event_data refusing a bad name, more than 1 KB, text that isn't JSON and JSON that isn't an object. With a World (checkClientEventsIn): client.<name> delivered to a subscriber with the sender's entity and data, the 10 a second limit, the dropped count and its log line at the despawn. checkMovableAndMapSpawn: kast.setMovable refusing moves with Correction and its errors, and kast.map.spawn; the Spawn built-in's pos / heading in the spawn checks.
  • Starter-mmo combat (checkStarterMmoCombat in lua_checks.cpp, compiled only when built in the repo, with KAST_STARTER_MMO pointing at samples/starter-mmo): loads the sample's Lua and maps and drives its systems/combat.lua, regen.lua, npcs.lua and fsms/ on the injected clock: refused attacks, a hit and the cooldown, the Guard hitting back for 5 every 2 s and returning home healed, the Bat killed, its corpse staying 60 s and the Bat respawning 30 s after it went, a character killed (its move corrected, its attack refused, no regeneration) and revived at the map spawn with full Health (a Warrior's 140: these characters have no class), walking not draining Health, the 30 m leash, and regeneration starting only 5 s after combat.
  • Starter-mmo loot (checkStarterMmoLoot, with KAST_STARTER_MMO_TEST_LUA pointing at cmake/starter_mmo_test_server.lua): a copy of the sample's server Lua plus that test-only system (seeded rolls, set bags) drives systems/inventory.lua: an empty 16-slot bag for a new character and a saved one cleaned on login; move, swap, merge up to maxStack, split and destroy; every malformed or illegal bag request refused with its reason and nothing changed; the tagger owning the corpse, another player refused, out of range refused, seed 1's roll (1 gold, Bat Wing, Linen Cloth), gold to the Wallet, a full bag leaving the loot in the corpse (full), stacking onto existing stacks, the emptied corpse closing and going, and the bag saved with the character. checkItemsAndLoot: every item and loot table definition error, duplicate ids, kast.items and kast.npcDef.
  • Character creation (self_check.cpp + checkCharacterCreation in lua_checks.cpp): the CharCreate / CharInfo options round trip and byte layout, a key twice or a cut option is malformed, the C++ limits (8 entries, keys of 1–16 [a-z_], values of up to 32 printable ASCII); without a validateCharacter only empty options pass; with hooks every one must return true, an error refuses and disables its system; kast.get(e, "Character").options.
  • Starter-mmo classes (checkStarterMmoClasses, a copy of the sample's server Lua with the test-only system): the sample's hook (exactly class, one of four); Class from the creation options, owner-only, a classless character becoming a saved Warrior; max Health 140 / 80 / 90 / 110; class.powers to the owner; every rejection (wrong class, unknown, malformed, target, range, GCD, cooldown, not behind, dead) changing nothing; each power's damage and range; Frost Nova rooting within 8 m (a chasing NPC stays put 4 s but still hits in reach, a patrol stops); Heal capped at max; Power Shield absorbing exactly 30 then breaking, or fading after 10 s; Shield Wall's halved hits for 6 s; Evasion dodging about half of 20 seeded hits and none after; a Mage's Firebolt tagging the Bat; revive at the class's max Health.
  • Maps (in lua_checks.cpp): tile math at the edges, every manifest and Maps::load error, bounds Correction, per-map AOI, World::transfer message order, map save/load fallbacks, and kast.transfer / Transform.map.
  • Areas (in lua_checks.cpp): rect, concave polygon, y range, priority and parent flag inheritance; every areas.json and portal load error; the sample maps loading. With the sample maps and lua/: area.enter on login (and events.lua's log line), area.leave then area.enter exactly once on crossing a border, and a walk into the cave portal (map, position, heading, events, MapChange before Despawn before Spawn in that tick, no EntityMove leaking to the old neighbours, the save on map 2). kast.map.area / areaAt for players, NPCs and points, and their errors.
  • NPC spawns (in lua_checks.cpp): parse_spawns with every field, default and rejection; every spawn file load error (file name, tile not on the map, pos outside the tile, duplicate id across a map's files); respawn on the injected clock (not back at 29.95 s, back at 30 s at its pos, -1 never), MAX_NPCS deferring with one warning; the Spawn built-in from Lua (query, fields, read-only). With the sample maps and lua/: the Guard and the Bat from their files, the Guard visiting its 4 path points at 3 m/s, and the test hook's destroy and 5 s respawn.
  • Ground (self_check.cpp movement + lua_checks.cpp): validate_move lifting under-ground moves, in_air for rising or hovering past MOVE_MAX_AIR (spending no budget), falling from 50 m; the two env vars. A generated ramp map loaded from .r16 files: Maps::ground at samples, between them, on tile edges, a tile without a file, off the map, and equal to map::ground; a bad heights file. With a World on it: entering under the ground, moves (lifted, Correction, falling), an NPC created and a spawn file at y = 100 standing on the ground, a transfer landing on it, and kast.map.ground. With the sample maps: hills' PNG heightmaps load.
  • Navmesh (lua_checks.cpp + ctest mapbake_cli): an in-memory bake of a 2-tile ramp with a 22 m block whose sides are 70 degree slopes: seam_errors on a torn edge, a path from tile 0_0 to tile 1_0 that goes round the block, no path to a point off the mesh, on_mesh false on the 70 degree slope and 20 m up, true on the ramp, the seam and 5 m up. Navtiles with a bad magic, navtile or Detour version, another tile's blob, or truncated: each a load error. Moves: onto the slope Correction, along the ramp accepted, MOVE_NAV_CHECK=0 accepted, a map without navtiles as in Ground; validate_move's off_mesh spends no budget. kast.map.path from Lua (points, ends, the detour, nil cases, errors). With the sample maps: hills on the mesh on a 32 degree slope (the client's 40 degree limit, not Recast's ledge filter's ~27) and off it on the mesa wall, and the Sentry walking round the mesa on the ground. mapbake_cli runs the tool: a torn seam and a spawn outside its tile exit 1 with nothing baked, and (in the repo) hills' stored navtiles equal a fresh bake.
  • Zone objects (lua_checks.cpp + self_check.cpp + ctest mapbake_cli): parse_objects on every field and each kind of bad entry (skipped, the rest kept), a repeated id and bad files; parse_interactables on every field, its defaults and each error, data more than 31 deep included; footprint_distance and footprint for boxes (turned by yaw) and cylinders. Maps::load: interactables by map and id, and each refusal (an id twice on a map, a bad file name, a tile not on the map, bad JSON, a bad entry). Interact: in range on the player's map publishes the event with {entity, map, object, data} (data nil when absent); another map's id, an unknown id and out of range publish nothing and log interact rejected at debug. An in-memory bake with a box wall, a cylinder interactable, a visual and a turned box over the tile seam: the blocking ones off the mesh (both tiles for the seam one), the visual walkable, a path round the wall, a move into it corrected. With the sample maps: the hills' obstacle crates off the mesh and its visual crate on it, the Test Shrine loaded, the Sentry walking round the crate on its route; starter-mmo's Meadow Shrine restoring Health and sending its blessing in range, nothing 20 m away. The codec round-trips Interact (version 5). mapbake_cli: an objects file with an unknown type and a blocking object without a shape exits 1 with both errors.
  • Integration (tests/integration.sh): drives kast_bot scenarios against the compose stack. Scenario 10a (persist) also checks that a new character enters map 1 and keeps its map across a relog.
    • The bots register, verify through Mailpit and log in with real auth tokens.
    • Scenarios 10–13 use docker compose restart/stop/start on world and mysql.
    • Scenario 14 uses the sample systems/events.lua: sendTo reaches only its target, sendNearby only bots in view, and broadcast every bot.
    • Scenario 15 (fsm) uses the sample locomotion machine. On entering, a bot gets exactly one fsm.state (nil->idle), because the denied idle->idle sends nothing. Walking, then standing still, gives idle->moving and then moving->idle. A bot in view gets none of them.
    • Scenario 16 (npcs) uses the sample systems/npcs.lua, npcs/guard.lua and the Guard's spawn file. A bot entering at the spawn point gets Spawn for the Guard (kind npc, template Guard), its Health from the definition (current 50, max 50) and EntityMoves at changing positions. Walking 200 m away gives Despawn and then nothing about it; walking back gives Spawn and the Health again.
    • Scenario 17 (portal) uses the sample areas.json files. Bot A walks from the spawn point into cave_mouth. It gets MapChange{2} at the portal destination before any Despawn/Spawn, then sees the Bat and loses bot B, who gets A's Despawn. After a relog A is still on map 2.
    • Scenario 18 (respawn) uses the sample systems/test_hooks.lua. Bot A sees the Guard; bot B enters as Respawntest..., and A gets the Guard's Despawn, then 4.5–6.5 s later a Spawn for a new Guard (new id, template Guard) at its spawn point.
    • Scenario 0 runs kast_mapbake in the image on the mounted hills (into /tmp; the maps are read-only) and logs the fresh and stored navtiles' checksums.
    • Scenario 19 (terrain) reads maps/hills itself (--map-root, the maps mounted into the bot). Bots A and B take hills_gate to map 3 and land on the ground. A walks 40 m down the slope (9 m, at most 36.5 degrees, under the navmesh's 40) at the ground's height with no Correction, and B's EntityMoves for A are within 5 cm of the ground. A rising 20 m then 30 m above the ground gets Correction back to its last position.
    • Scenario 20 (navmesh) takes bot A to the hills: walking at ground height to the mesa's south foot gets no Correction; walking on up its wall does. Over 30 s, the Sentry's EntityMoves pass the mesa's x range, all on the ground and at most 0.5 m into its walls' foot (a navmesh corner may graze it).
    • Scenario 21 (client-events) uses the sample systems/test_hooks.lua, which answers every client.test.echo with test.echo {n, entity}: a ClientEvent comes back with the sender's entity and its n. A name with capitals, a 33-character name, a payload over 1 KB, a JSON array and text that isn't JSON each get Disconnect{malformed}.
    • Scenario 22 (client-event-rate): 30 events at once give only the first 10 echoes, and a second later events get through again. The script then checks the world's log for "client events dropped" with count 20.
    • Scenario 25 (objects) uses the hills' zone object fixtures and the sample systems/test_hooks.lua, which answers test.shrine with a ServerEvent carrying the payload. Interact{1} on map 1, at the hills' portal arrival (11 m from the shrine's edge) and Interact{999} get nothing; walked to 1.9 m from it, Interact{1} gets the answer with {entity, map 3, object 1, msg hello}; walking into the obstacle crate gets a Correction outside it.
    • tests/integration.sh --starter-mmo runs only scenarios 23 and 24, against the stack started with the sample's override (docker compose -f docker-compose.yml -f ../../samples/starter-mmo/docker-compose.override.yml up -d --build), using samples/starter-mmo/systems/combat.lua; its bots are Warriors (--char-option class=warrior) and attack with Strike (combat.power {power = "strike"}, 12 damage):
      • Scenario 23 (combat-bat): a bot walks through the Cavern Gate; an attack on the Bat from 8 m gets combat.rejected {reason = range}; from 2.5 m one hit (12) gives combat.power, combat.hit and combat.death; it stays as the bot's corpse (Corpse {owner}, no Despawn); loot.open gives loot.contents with 1–3 gold, loot.take_all empties it (loot.closed, Despawn); a new Bat spawns at its spawn point 29–32 s after that.
      • Scenario 24 (combat-rules): a player, a string and an unknown id as targets get {reason = target}; next to the Guard two attacks at once give one hit and {reason = cooldown}; after the cooldown a hit again, the Guard hits back for 5 and its Health replicates down to 26; 10 m off an attack gets {reason = range}.
    • Logs go to build/integration-<run>/.
  • Load (tests/load.sh):
    • Setup: restarts world so its totals cover only the test. It then runs COUNT bots in one process (8 io threads, 50 new connections per second) for DURATION s.
    • Pass criteria:
      • Bot summary PASS: every bot entered the world, with no errors, no unexpected disconnects and no Correction for honest movers. A wanderer that walks into the sample portal stops on the other map (map_changes_recv); the corrections for its moves still in flight are not counted.
      • Every 10 s window's tick p99 is under 25 ms, and so is the run total.
      • Zero failed save attempts.
