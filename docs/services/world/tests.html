Tests

  • Self-check (tests/self_check.cpp + tests/lua_checks.cpp, ctest world_self_check): assert-style checks of the pure logic with an injected clock, so nothing sleeps. The Lua checks write their scripts to a temp folder and drive a World with captured sends, saves and log lines. They cover the event bus (the engine's cases), kast.publish/subscribe/unsubscribe and the client sends: audiences, order, rejections and timing. They also cover state machines, from definitions to C++ / Lua parity.
  • Maps (in lua_checks.cpp): tile math at the edges, every manifest and Maps::load error, bounds Correction, per-map AOI, World::transfer message order, map save/load fallbacks, and kast.transfer / Transform.map.
  • Areas (in lua_checks.cpp): rect, concave polygon, y range, priority and parent flag inheritance; every areas.json and portal load error; the sample maps loading. With the sample maps and lua/: area.enter on login (and events.lua's log line), area.leave then area.enter exactly once on crossing a border, and a walk into the cave portal (map, position, heading, events, MapChange before Despawn before Spawn in that tick, no EntityMove leaking to the old neighbours, the save on map 2). kast.map.area / areaAt for players, NPCs and points, and their errors.
  • NPC spawns (in lua_checks.cpp): parse_spawns with every field, default and rejection; every spawn file load error (file name, tile not on the map, pos outside the tile, duplicate id across a map's files); respawn on the injected clock (not back at 29.95 s, back at 30 s at its pos, -1 never), MAX_NPCS deferring with one warning; the Spawn built-in from Lua (query, fields, read-only). With the sample maps and lua/: the Guard and the Bat from their files, the Guard visiting its 4 path points at 3 m/s, and the test hook's destroy and 5 s respawn.
  • Ground (self_check.cpp movement + lua_checks.cpp): validate_move lifting under-ground moves, in_air for rising or hovering past MOVE_MAX_AIR (spending no budget), falling from 50 m; the two env vars. A generated ramp map loaded from .r16 files: Maps::ground at samples, between them, on tile edges, a tile without a file, off the map, and equal to map::ground; a bad heights file. With a World on it: entering under the ground, moves (lifted, Correction, falling), an NPC created and a spawn file at y = 100 standing on the ground, a transfer landing on it, and kast.map.ground. With the sample maps: hills' PNG heightmaps load.
  • Navmesh (lua_checks.cpp + ctest mapbake_cli): an in-memory bake of a 2-tile ramp with a 22 m block whose sides are 70 degree slopes: seam_errors on a torn edge, a path from tile 0_0 to tile 1_0 that goes round the block, no path to a point off the mesh, on_mesh false on the 70 degree slope and 20 m up, true on the ramp, the seam and 5 m up. Navtiles with a bad magic, navtile or Detour version, another tile's blob, or truncated: each a load error. Moves: onto the slope Correction, along the ramp accepted, MOVE_NAV_CHECK=0 accepted, a map without navtiles as in Ground; validate_move's off_mesh spends no budget. kast.map.path from Lua (points, ends, the detour, nil cases, errors). With the sample maps: hills on the mesh on a 32 degree slope (the client's 40 degree limit, not Recast's ledge filter's ~27) and off it on the mesa wall, and the Sentry walking round the mesa on the ground. mapbake_cli runs the tool: a torn seam and a spawn outside its tile exit 1 with nothing baked, and (in the repo) hills' stored navtiles equal a fresh bake.
  • Integration (tests/integration.sh): drives kast_bot scenarios against the compose stack. Scenario 10a (persist) also checks that a new character enters map 1 and keeps its map across a relog.
    • The bots register, verify through Mailpit and log in with real auth tokens.
    • Scenarios 10–13 use docker compose restart/stop/start on world and mysql.
    • Scenario 14 uses the sample systems/events.lua: sendTo reaches only its target, sendNearby only bots in view, and broadcast every bot.
    • Scenario 15 (fsm) uses the sample locomotion machine. On entering, a bot gets exactly one fsm.state (nil->idle), because the denied idle->idle sends nothing. Walking, then standing still, gives idle->moving and then moving->idle. A bot in view gets none of them.
    • Scenario 16 (npcs) uses the sample systems/npcs.lua, npcs/guard.lua and the Guard's spawn file. A bot entering at the spawn point gets Spawn for the Guard (kind npc, template Guard), its Health from the definition (current 50, max 50) and EntityMoves at changing positions. Walking 200 m away gives Despawn and then nothing about it; walking back gives Spawn and the Health again.
    • Scenario 17 (portal) uses the sample areas.json files. Bot A walks from the spawn point into cave_mouth. It gets MapChange{2} at the portal destination before any Despawn/Spawn, then sees the Bat and loses bot B, who gets A's Despawn. After a relog A is still on map 2.
    • Scenario 18 (respawn) uses the sample systems/test_hooks.lua. Bot A sees the Guard; bot B enters as Respawntest..., and A gets the Guard's Despawn, then 4.5–6.5 s later a Spawn for a new Guard (new id, template Guard) at its spawn point.
    • Scenario 0 runs kast_mapbake in the image on the mounted hills (into /tmp; the maps are read-only) and logs the fresh and stored navtiles' checksums.
    • Scenario 19 (terrain) reads maps/hills itself (--map-root, the maps mounted into the bot). Bots A and B take hills_gate to map 3 and land on the ground. A walks 40 m down the slope (9 m, at most 36.5 degrees, under the navmesh's 40) at the ground's height with no Correction, and B's EntityMoves for A are within 5 cm of the ground. A rising 20 m then 30 m above the ground gets Correction back to its last position.
    • Scenario 20 (navmesh) takes bot A to the hills: walking at ground height to the mesa's south foot gets no Correction; walking on up its wall does. Over 30 s, the Sentry's EntityMoves pass the mesa's x range, all on the ground and at most 0.5 m into its walls' foot (a navmesh corner may graze it).
    • Logs go to build/integration-<run>/.
  • Load (tests/load.sh):
    • Setup: restarts world so its totals cover only the test. It then runs COUNT bots in one process (8 io threads, 50 new connections per second) for DURATION s.
    • Pass criteria:
      • Bot summary PASS: every bot entered the world, with no errors, no unexpected disconnects and no Correction for honest movers. A wanderer that walks into the sample portal stops on the other map (map_changes_recv); the corrections for its moves still in flight are not counted.
      • Every 10 s window's tick p99 is under 25 ms, and so is the run total.
      • Zero failed save attempts.
