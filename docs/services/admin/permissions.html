Permissions

Three roles, taken from auth's access token: player, gm, admin. Players cannot sign in. The admin service gates every route by role so the UI gets clean 403 forbidden answers, and auth re-checks account and realm calls with the caller's own token. The SPA hides what a role cannot do, but it is not the security boundary.

Side effects

  • Ban and role change kick the account's online characters on every reachable realm. Kick failures don't block the change; they are returned (kicks.failures) and audited.
  • Delete account kicks everywhere, deletes the account in auth (bans and tokens cascade), then soft-deletes all its characters (deleted_at).
