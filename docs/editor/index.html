Kast Editor

kast_editor is the Unity-style editor built on the engine with Dear ImGui docking and ImGuizmo. Without --project, --scene, --save-scene or --build it opens the Project Manager. Build and start it as described in the repository README.

Scene settings panels

Hidden until opened from the Window menu; edits show live in the Scene and Game views and mark the scene dirty (* in the title).

  • Lighting: Ambient color and intensity (Coordinates and scenes), and Sky: a Mode combo (Off, Procedural, Cubemap), the Top, Horizon and Bottom colors (shown for Procedural and Cubemap: they are the cubemap's fallback) and, for Cubemap, a picker per face (+X (px) .. -Z (nz)) listing the project's PNG and JPG files. When a face can't be used the panel says why in red and the views show the gradient (Skybox). With a sky on, the sky also lights the meshes (Image-based lighting). Then Lightmaps (Lightmaps and GI): Enabled, Intensity and Directional (apply at once), Texels / Meter, Bounces, Samples / Texel and Probe Spacing (m) (apply at the next bake), all undoable; the bake's status (Not baked, Baked, Out of date in #ffcc00, or in red why its files can't be used) and Bake Lightmaps, which bakes on worker threads while the editor keeps drawing: a progress bar and Cancel replace the button until it is done (disabled during Play, until the scene is saved in a project, or while no mesh is Static and no Terrain has Bake Lightmap; the tooltip says why). Then VoxelGI (VoxelGI): each VoxelGI volume's name and bake status (as its Inspector shows it, logged as VoxelGI '<name>': <status> when it changes) and Bake VoxelGI, which voxelizes every volume on a worker thread while the editor keeps drawing: a progress bar and Cancel replace the button until it is done (disabled during Play, without volumes, or until the scene is saved in a project). Then SDFGI (SDFGI): Enabled, Energy, Bounce Feedback (0 to 1), a Quality combo (Low, Medium, High), Cascades (1 to 4), Cell Size (m) (0.125 to 4, the first cascade's) and a Y Scale combo (1, 0.5, 0.25: flatter cascades for open worlds). There is nothing to bake: every field applies at once in the Scene and Game views, each an undoable edit. Then Reflection Probes: how many the scene has and Bake All Probes, which bakes each one as its Inspector's Bake does (Reflection probes; disabled during Play, without probes, or until the scene is saved in a project).
  • Post Processing: the post effects and fog (Post-processing and fog).
  • Physics: gravity (Physics).
