VoxelGI volumes

A VoxelGI volume gives everything inside its box diffuse global illumination and glossy reflections that follow the lights in real time: move a lamp, swing a door's light, change the time of day, and the red wall's glow on the floor moves with it, without a re-bake. The room's Static geometry is voxelized once in the editor; every frame the GPU relights those voxels with the scene's lights (their shadow maps occluding), bounces the light, and meshes cone-trace the result (engine/voxel_gi.h, engine/shaders/cs_voxelgi_*.sc, engine/shaders/fs_mesh.sc). The Scene view, the Game view and the built game render it alike.

Nothing changes until you add a volume: a scene without one renders pixel for pixel as before.

Quick start

  1. Set the room's walls, floor, ceiling and fixed props to GI Mode Static (their Mesh Renderer or Model Renderer; Lightmaps and GI). Static is what the volume voxelizes: what bounces and blocks its light.
  2. Create an empty entity in the middle of the room, Add Component > Voxel GI and size its Box Size to just enclose the room (the orange box in the Scene view).
  3. Save the scene in a project and press the volume's Bake (Inspector) or Window > Lighting > Bake VoxelGI. It takes a fraction of a second; the scene is marked dirty: save it.
  4. Everything inside the box now takes its ambient light from the volume. Lights can move; Static meshes can't (move one and the status says Out of date: bake again).

Intensity, quality, priority and enabled apply at once; box size, resolution and the volume's position at the next bake (until then the old bake keeps rendering where it was baked and the status says Out of date; a bake of another resolution than the volume's can't be used at all).

Baking

The bake gathers the scene as Bake Lightmaps does (Static primitives and non-skinned models in their bind pose, and the tiles of every Terrain with Bake Lightmap on), then samples every triangle touching the box at half a voxel's spacing; each voxel a sample falls in averages its surfaces' albedo (base color times its texture's average times 1 - metallic), interpolated normal and emission. It is deterministic and runs on a worker thread: the editor keeps drawing, with a progress bar and Cancel in the Lighting panel and the Inspector. tests/project/voxelgi_room.scene (a 6.6 m room) bakes in 0.03 s at resolution 128 (119 000 voxels) and in 0.15 s at 256 (483 000 voxels). Command line: --bake-voxelgi.

Files

<scene-dir>/<scene-name>_voxelgi/<id>.kvox   the voxels: "KVOX", version, grid size, count, then per voxel 16 bytes:
                                            index, albedo RGBA8 (a: opacity), normal RGB8, emission RGBE8
<scene-dir>/<scene-name>_voxelgi/<id>.json   version, hash, resolution, grid (origin, voxel size, dims), voxel count,
                                            the .kvox's name and FNV-1a hash

Only voxels a surface touches are stored, so a file grows with the surface area, not with the box's volume: 1.9 MB for the test room at 128, 7.7 MB at 256 (a dense 2563 RGBA8 volume would be 64 MB per channel). Both files are written atomically, the json last, so a cancelled or failed bake leaves the previous one intact. The hash covers every Static mesh (mesh, material, world matrix), lightmapped terrain and the volume's position, box and resolution. Build ships both files in game.pak; the player loads them like the editor.

Every frame

Each enabled, baked volume runs three compute passes in the VoxelGI view, after the frame's shadow maps (the last of Renderer::kShadowViews):

  1. Inject (cs_voxelgi_inject.sc, one thread per baked voxel): the directional light and the local lights nearest the volume that reach it (point, spot and area; 16 lights in all) light each voxel exactly as fs_mesh.sc lights a rough surface (pointLightFalloff, spotConeFalloff, the area light's form factor and range window; the C++ mirror voxelGiLightIrradiance is checked against them in kast_self_check), each shadowed by its shadow map of this frame (one hardware-compared tap; points outside the directional light's cascades count as lit). A voxel's light is its albedo times that plus its emission.
  2. Mips (cs_voxelgi_mip.sc): an RGBA16F 3D radiance texture (rgb premultiplied by opacity in a) gets its isotropic mip chain, each level the mean of the 2 x 2 x 2 voxels under it.
  3. Bounces (cs_voxelgi_bounce.sc): every voxel gathers the mipped volume with the preset's diffuse cones and writes its direct light plus albedo times what it gathered into a second volume (ping-pong), mipped again: one bounce at Low and Medium, two at High.

Shading

A draw whose world bounds' center is inside a volume's box cone-traces its final radiance volume (kastVoxelDiffuse, kastVoxelSpecular in fs_mesh.sc):

diffuse  = sum over the preset's cones of weight * (cone light + (1 - cone occlusion) * environment(cone direction))
specular = (cone light + (1 - cone occlusion) * environment cube) * (F0 * brdf.x + brdf.y)   one cone along the reflection,
           tan(half angle) = max(roughness^2, 0.04)
both     * intensity, faded into the light the mesh gets without the volume over the box's last 4 voxels

Cones march front to back, each step's mip as wide as the cone there. A mip voxel's opacity is scaled by its size in voxels (so a voxel-thin wall blocks a wide cone as a solid one would) and each sample is lifted off the surface by the cone's width (so the surface's own voxels don't shade it). What a cone sees past the volume unblocked is the environment the mesh would get anyway: the sky's or a reflection probe's light, or the flat ambient.

The diffuse follows the precedence Lightmap > VoxelGI > SDFGI > probe grid > environment: a Static mesh with a valid lightmap keeps the lightmap's diffuse; every other mesh in the box (Off, Dynamic, a Static one the lightmaps don't cover) takes the volume's, fading at the box's faces into the probe grid (if it samples one) or the environment (not into SDFGI: a mesh is in a volume or it isn't, by its bounds' center; with SDFGI on, the meshes outside every box take SDFGI). The specular inside the box is the volume's for every mesh, lightmapped ones included: it replaces the reflection probe's and the sky's.

Dynamic meshes are lit by the volume but are never voxelized: they don't bounce its light or block it (their shadows in the shadow maps do darken what inject lights).

Overlapping volumes

A draw takes one volume, chosen as reflection probes are (selectProbe): of the volumes whose box contains its bounds' center, the highest priority; among equals the nearest box center; then scene order. Put a small high-priority volume in an alcove of a big room's volume to give it finer voxels.

When a bake can't be used

Not baked, a missing or corrupt .json or .kvox, a .kvox that doesn't match its hash, another version, or a bake of another resolution than the volume's: the engine warns once (VoxelGI 'Room': 'room_voxelgi/13.json': baked at resolution 128, the volume is 64 (bake again); meshes in its box are lit as outside it), the Inspector and the Lighting panel show why, and the meshes in its box get the light below VoxelGI in the precedence, exactly as without the volume. A GPU without compute shaders or RGBA16F 3D images turns VoxelGI off with one warning. Never a crash, never black.

Lua

A volume's fields are ordinary component fields: read and set them with kast.get / kast.set. Intensity and enabled apply at once; there is no Lua bake (changing the box or resolution only makes the bake out of date or unusable).

for e in kast.query("VoxelGI") do
    kast.set(e, "VoxelGI", {intensity = 0.5})   -- dusk: half the bounced light
end
kast.set(room, "VoxelGI", {enabled = false})    -- this volume off: the probe grid or the sky instead

Limits

  • Isotropic mips: light leaks through walls thinner than about two voxels at coarse mips, and a mip voxel's color is the mean of all its sides. Six anisotropic volumes would fix both, at six times the memory and mip cost.
  • Shadows come from this frame's shadow maps: the directional light's cascades follow the camera, so voxels outside them count as lit, and only the shadowed point, spot and area lights nearest the camera have maps at all.
  • Static means static: moving geometry neither bounces nor blocks the volume's light until the next bake. The sky's light isn't bounced (cones that leave the volume see it directly), only the lights'.
  • At most 16 lights relight a volume; every volume relights every frame (no time slicing); D3D11 only, as the rest of the renderer.
  • .kshader materials with two or more texture2D params give VoxelGI's sampler stage (11) to their second one: their draws get the light below VoxelGI.
